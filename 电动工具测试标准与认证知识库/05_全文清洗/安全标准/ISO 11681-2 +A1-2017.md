Introduction
This Amendment introduces extra information at the point of sale of chain-saws for tree service and
further clarifies that the product is intended to be used by trained operators only.
This issue was brought up by market surveillance authorities in Europe and is related to the availability
of this product to the consumer market.

Machinery for forestry — Portable chain-saw safety
requirements and testing —
Part 2:
Chain-saws for tree service
AMENDMENT 1
Add the following normative reference:
Page 1, Clause 2
ISO 7010, Graphical symbols — Safety colours and safety signs — Registered safety signs
Replace the text “See also 5.4.4” with “See also 5.5”.
Page 16, 5.3, second paragraph
Delete the whole subclause.
Page 18, 5.4.4
Add the following new subclause:
Page 18
The chain-saw shall be provided with a hangtag or a label affixed to the machine and the packaging
carton shall incorporate a pre-printed text or an adhesive label providing the following verbatim text,
in the official language of the country of sale, clearly visible to the potential purchaser of the chain-saw:
5.5
Warning to be provided at point of sale
WARNING — This chain-saw is for use by trained tree service operators only. Use without proper
training can result in severe injuries.
Where a label is affixed to the machine, it may be of a type that can be removed.
The word WARNING shall be in black capital letters on yellow or orange background and the letters
used in the text after the word WARNING shall be black on white background.
Safety sign W001 of ISO 7010 shall precede the warnings for each language.
The safety sign W001 of ISO 7010 shall be black on yellow background.
When printed on the packaging carton, the safety sign and wording need not be in accordance with the
above colour requirements, provided they are in contrast to the background and are incorporated on at
least two faces of the carton, excluding the bottom face.
The safety sign shall have at least the same height as the word WARNING. As an alternative, one safety
sign in common for all languages may be used. If so, the minimum height of the safety sign shall be 15 mm.
On hangtags or labels attached to the chain-saw, the minimum height of letters used in the word
WARNING shall be 2,5 mm. The letters used in the text after the word WARNING shall have a minimum
height of 2,0 mm.
