Text of All to EN 60745-1:2009
19 Mechanical hazards
19.1 Add after the 2nd paragraph:
For fixed guards that are to be removed at the place of use as part of the routine maintenance
procedure, as described in the instruction manual, the fastenings shall remain attached to the guard or
to the tool.
NOTE 1 Replacement of brushes is not considered as part of the routine maintenance.
Replace 'NOTE 1' and 'NOTE 2' by 'NOTE 2' and 'NOTE 3' respectively.
