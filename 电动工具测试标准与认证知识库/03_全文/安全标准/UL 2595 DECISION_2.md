UNDERWRITERS LABORATORIES INC. CERTIFICATION REQUIREMENT DECISION
This Certiﬁcation Requirement Decision is prepared by UL LLC. It is normative for the applicable UL
Product Certiﬁcation Program(s); however, it is currently not part of the UL Standard(s) referenced
below.
Product Category (CCN): BBOI / BBON
Standard Number: UL 2595
Standard Title: Standard for General Requirements for Battery-Powered Appliances
Edition Date: September 9, 2015
Edition Number: 2
Section / Paragraph Reference: 20.2
Subject: Risk of Injury due to Starting of the Appliance
DECISION:
20.2 For parts of different polarity, clearance and creepage distances less than those given in Table 20.1
are acceptable if the shorting of the two parts does not result in a risk of injury due to the appliance starting
or in a risk of ﬁre as speciﬁed in 11.1.6(f). The risk of ﬁre due to spacings below the required values is
covered by the requirements of Abnormal Operation - Charging Systems, Clause 11.1.
RATIONALE FOR DECISION:
Unexpected starting of the appliance will not introduce a hazard unless it results in a risk of injury, such
as due to an exposed moving part capable of causing injury. This CRD is intended to clarify Clause 20.2
to indicate that clearance and creepage distances less than those speciﬁed in Table 20.1 are acceptable
if shorting of those spacings does not result in a risk of injury or ﬁre.
