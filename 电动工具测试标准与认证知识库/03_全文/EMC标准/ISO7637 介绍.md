What is EMC? - Special Considerations for Motor Vehicles
什么是EMC? – 针对汽车的特殊考虑
Causes 原因:
 EMC is the ability of a device or 
system to function without error in its 
intended electromagnetic environment. 
单体或系统在电磁环境下工作
 Alternators 发电器, Converters 转换器, 
Switching Processes 开关工作过程。
 Electric Motors, Fuel Pump, Fan Motors 电马
达，汽油泵，风扇马达
 In ISO 7637 and variants, we are 
considering transients and voltage 
drops that are coupled over battery 
lines and signal lines ISO7637及其它的
衍生标准，我们考虑的通过电源线及信号

 Lights, Radio 灯，收音机

Harnesses 通过电源线和线束线缆耦合
 Engine Start 引擎启动

Overview
Pulse 1 - A simulation of transients due to  supply disconnection from inductive loads;  
it applies to a DUT if as  used in the vehicle, it remains connected directly in parallel 
with an inductive load
波形1模拟了电源从感性负载断开时，与感性负载并联的被测件上所产生的瞬变脉冲。
Pulse 2a - Simulates transients due to sudden  interruption of currents in a device 
connected in parallel with the  DUT  due to the inductances of the wiring harness
波形2a模拟了并联到DUT上的设备中的电流突然中断时，由于线束自感产生的脉冲。
Pulse 2b - Simulates transients from dc  motors acting as generators after the ignition 
is switched off
2b波形模拟了点火电路关闭后直流马达产生的瞬变波形。
Pulse 3a/3b - Occurs as the result of switching processes. The characteristics of this 
pulse are influenced by distributed capacitance and inductance of the wiring harness
波形3是由于开关动作引起的。线缆的分布电容/电感会影响脉冲波形的特性
Magnetic Field Immunity – Simulates magnetic fields 
generated by electric motors, daytime running lamps, etc. 
for DUTs with magnetically sensitive devices.
磁场抗扰度– 马达，车灯等产生的磁场，对一些DUT造成
干扰。
Transformer Coupled Sine Waves – Sinusoidal noise burst 
coupled on battery lines
变压器耦合正弦波– 耦合到电池线路上的正弦干扰
